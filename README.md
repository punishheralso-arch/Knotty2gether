# Knotty2gether

**A Proprietary Alchemical Craftsmanship Experience**

## Vision Statement

Knotty2gether is an exclusive, invitation-only experiential service designed to bring couples together through the transformative art of crafting bullwhips. This experience combines alchemy, transmutation, and intentional craftsmanship in curated, controlled environments. Every element—from the dark room aesthetics to the vises holding the whips—is designed with purpose.

---

## Experience Offerings

### Standard Couples Experience
- **Duration**: Three 2-hour sessions (6 hours total)
- **Format**: Couples only
- **Setting**: Darkroom environment with black light, tables with vices for whip braiding
- **Price**: $500.00 per couple (all-inclusive)
- **Progression**: Video confirmation required at each stage to ensure adherence to guidelines
- **Output**: Completed handcrafted bullwhip

### Premium Nudist Experience (Matching Available)
- **Duration**: 30-minute sessions
- **Format**: Nudist only section
- **Price**: $1,500.00 per session
- **Features**:
  - Same alchemical crafting environment
  - Matching wheel option for solo participants (randomly pairs with live or online participants)
  - Braiding done with electrical tape
  - Exclusive aesthetic and experience guidelines
