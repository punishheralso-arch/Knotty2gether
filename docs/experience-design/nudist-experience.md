## The Matching Wheel System

### Purpose

The Matching Wheel provides solo participants with an opportunity to be randomly paired with another compatible participant (live or online), transforming a solo experience into a shared journey.

### How It Works

#### For Solo Participants (Live)
1. **Booking**: Solo individual books a nudist session
2. **Matching Request**: Indicates desire to be matched with another participant
3. **Wheel Activation**: Creator spins matching wheel (live in-session or pre-session)
4. **Pairing**: Solo participant is paired with another available solo or with a specific online partner
5. **Experience**: Collaborative electrical tape braiding with matched partner

#### For Online Participants
1. **Remote Booking**: Individual books nudist experience from remote location
2. **Video Setup**: Secure video conferencing with end-to-end encryption
3. **Wheel Matching**: May be matched with live in-studio participant or another online participant
4. **Synchronized Crafting**: Both participants braid simultaneously, visible to each other via video
5. **Shared Completion**: Experience completion and integration via video connection

### Matching Algorithm

Pairing considers:
- **Availability**: Compatible session times
- **Experience Level**: Similar braiding skill or mutual beginners
- **Energy Match**: Personality and intention compatibility (when possible)
- **Randomness**: Ultimate selection is randomized via the wheel
- **Consent**: Both parties must consent to matching before pairing is final

### Wheel Mechanics

- **Physical Wheel**: Large spinning wheel with participant names/profiles
- **Digital Alternative**: Random algorithm for remote/online matching
- **Transparency**: Process is witnessed and recorded for authenticity
- **Fate Element**: Incorporates chance, vulnerability, and surrender to connection

## Materials & Technique

### Electrical Tape Braiding

**Why Electrical Tape?**
- Sustainable, different material than standard whip braiding
- Unique tactile experience
- Faster completion in 30-minute timeframe
- Colorful, visually distinct in black light

**Braiding Process**
1. **Tape Preparation**: Multiple colors pre-cut and organized

3. **Tension**: Managing tape tension without traditional vices (nude-friendly adjustment)
4. **Completion**: Finishing and securing the taped braid
5. **Final Product**: A colorful, flexible electrical tape braid (kept by participants or creator's choice)
