# Couples Experience: Knotty2gether Standard Offering

## Overview

The Knotty2gether couples experience is a transformative 6-hour journey designed specifically for partners seeking to deepen their connection through collaborative craftsmanship, alchemy, and intentional presence.

---

## Experience Specifications

### Duration & Structure
- **Total Time**: 6 hours
- **Format**: Three consecutive 2-hour sessions
- **Pricing**: $500.00 per couple (all-inclusive)
- **Participant Requirement**: Couples only (2 people per booking)
- **Frequency**: By appointment only

### Environment Requirements

#### Lighting & Atmosphere
- **Primary Setting**: Darkroom environment
- **Lighting Type**: Black light exclusively
- **Purpose**: To create altered sensory experience and focus participants on tactile connection and the work itself
- **Sound**: To be determined (ambient, silence, or guided audio)

#### Equipment & Setup
- **Table Configuration**: Sturdy workstations positioned for two
- **Vices**: Individual vices for each participant to secure braiding materials
- **Purpose**: Hands-free securing allows focus on technique and partner synchronization
- **Materials**: Professional-grade whip braiding materials + electrical tape alternatives for certain sections

#### Spatial Design
- **Room Layout**: Secluded, dedicated space with no external distractions
- **Access Control**: Entry/exit monitored to maintain immersion
- **Aesthetic**: Minimal, intentional, focused entirely on the experience

---

## Session Breakdown

### Session 1: Foundation & Initiation (Hours 1-2)

**Objective**: Establish connection, introduce materials, begin foundational techniques

#### Content
1. **Welcome & Intention Setting** (15 minutes)
   - Participants establish shared intention for the experience
   - Guidelines review and safety briefing
   - Introduction to the alchemy of material transformation

2. **Material Exploration** (30 minutes)
   - Hands-on introduction to braiding materials
   - Understanding tension, texture, and technique
   - Synchronized breathing and movement practice

3. **Foundational Braiding Techniques** (45 minutes)
   - Basic weave patterns
   - Vice positioning and material securing
   - Partner coordination and communication

4. **First Completion Milestone** (30 minutes)
   - Complete first braided section as a unit
   - Recognition and pause for integration

#### Video Confirmation Checkpoint
- Creator reviews video of Session 1 execution
- Verifies adherence to guidelines and technique
- Provides feedback before Session 2 clearance

---

### Session 2: Deepening & Complexity (Hours 3-4)

**Objective**: Build skill, increase complexity, deepen partnership synchronization

#### Content
1. **Integration & Reflection** (15 minutes)
   - Recap of Session 1 insights
   - Connection/intention renewal

2. **Advanced Weave Patterns** (45 minutes)
   - More intricate braiding techniques
   - Color/material variation introduction
   - Enhanced partner coordination

3. **Collaborative Momentum** (45 minutes)
   - Extended braiding in unified rhythm
   - Witnessing the transmutation of materials into craft

4. **Transition Preparation** (15 minutes)
   - Pause and integration
   - Preparation for final session

#### Video Confirmation Checkpoint
- Creator reviews Session 2 video
- Ensures continued adherence and skill progression
- Clears for final session

---

### Session 3: Completion & Transmutation (Hours 5-6)

**Objective**: Finalize the bullwhip, complete the alchemy, integrate the experience

#### Content
1. **Centering & Closure Preparation** (15 minutes)
   - Grounding exercise
   - Final intention alignment

2. **Final Braiding Completion** (60 minutes)
   - Complete the bullwhip using all learned techniques
   - Maintain synchronized rhythm throughout
   - Experience the full transformation from raw materials to finished craft

3. **Whip Finishing** (30 minutes)
   - Handle wrapping
   - Final adjustments
   - Quality verification

4. **Ceremonial Completion & Integration** (15 minutes)
   - Present the completed whip
   - Shared reflection on the journey
   - Exchange of acknowledgment/gratitude
   - Discussion of ongoing integration

#### Video Confirmation Checkpoint
- Creator reviews final session
- Verifies completion quality and guideline adherence
- Provides completion certification

## Guidelines & Requirements

### Mandatory Adherence

Participants must:
- Maintain focus and presence throughout all sessions
- Follow all instructions regarding material handling
- Respect the darkroom environment (no flashlights, screens, external light)
- Maintain synchronization with partner
- Comply with all safety protocols

### Video Confirmation Protocol

- **Recording**: All three sessions are recorded by the creator or authorized staff
- **Review**: Creator reviews each session before clearance to next
- **Criteria for Clearance**:
  - Proper technique execution
  - Safety compliance
  - Partnership coordination
  - Adherence to experiential guidelines
  - Material handling proficiency

### Confidentiality

- No external recording, photography, or audio
- No public sharing of experience details
- No replication or adaptation of techniques
- No disclosure of environment design, pricing, or business model
